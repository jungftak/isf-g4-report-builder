How this tool works

  1. Add every student in your class on the Class Roster tab (legal first name + pronoun).
  2. Go to Comment Builder, pick a student, and build their Pastoral, English, and Guided Discovery comments by checking statements from the official bank.
  3. English and Guided Discovery statements are locked exactly as approved — you only choose which ones apply. The name and pronoun are substituted automatically.
  4. Aim for 3–4 strengths and 2–3 growth statements per subject (5–7 total). You'll see a warning (not a hard block) if you go outside that.
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the Comment Builder to see the Final PowerSchool-Ready Preview — a one-stop summary of all 5 fields for the selected student, with a dedicated Copy button for each.
  6. Use the ⬇ Export All (Word) button at the top to download every student's comments in one neatly formatted document.

⚠️ Where is my data stored?

Everything you type is saved only in this browser, on this computer (nothing is sent to a server, and other teachers cannot see your students even if they open the same website on their own device). This means:

  • If you clear your browser history/cache, your work could be lost — use ⚙ Data → Backup regularly to download a safety copy.
  • If you switch computers, use Backup on the old one and Restore on the new one to carry your work over.
  • Do not use private/incognito windows — data won't be saved after you close the window.
